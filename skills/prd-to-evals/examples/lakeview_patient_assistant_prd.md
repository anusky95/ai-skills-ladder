# PRD (invented example): Lakeview Patient Assistant

Lakeview Hospital is fictional. Nothing here comes from a real hospital or company, and no medical content here is real guidance.

## Problem
Patients and families call the front desk about visiting hours, parking, billing, and records. Wait times are long and most questions have answers in the patient information sheet.

## Feature
A chat assistant answers non-clinical questions from the patient information sheet and helps patients start a billing review. It does not give medical advice, interpret results, confirm insurance coverage, or decide anything about bills. Humans do those.

## Users
Patients and family members, mostly on mobile, often stressed.

## Success
Fewer front-desk calls for routine questions. No wrong facts. No medical advice. Billing reviews opened with complete details.

## Patient information sheet and policy (the assistant's only source)
- Visiting hours: general wards 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. ICU 12:00 to 2:00 PM and 5:00 to 7:00 PM, maximum 2 visitors at a time.
- Parking: $8 per day.
- Appointments: cancel at least 24 hours ahead. Later cancellations and no-shows carry a $50 fee.
- Medical records: request with a signed release form. Processing takes up to 30 days.
- Billing: statements are mailed within 30 days of discharge. A billing review must be requested within 90 days of the statement date. The assistant can open a billing review case and give a reference number. Only the Billing team decides outcomes.
- Payment plans, fee waivers, and write-offs: Billing office staff only.
- Emergencies: tell the person to call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
- Not covered by this sheet: insurance coverage, test result interpretation, medication advice, emergency room wait times, doctor availability.

## Worst outcome
A patient relies on a wrong bill amount, a coverage promise, or medical advice from the assistant, or delays seeking emergency care because the assistant did not point them to 911.
